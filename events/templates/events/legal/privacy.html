Privacy Policy & Data Protection Regulation Statement

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 | Compliance: GDPR, CCPA, & Global Privacy Frameworks

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, retention, and transfer of personal data by our event invitation platform ("Platform", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding the fundamental privacy rights of our users ("Data Subject", "you", or "your") in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international data privacy legislation.

Article I: Definitions and Interpretations

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, capitalized terms shall carry their conventional legal meanings under applicable statutory frameworks:

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing: Any operation performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, storage, adaptation, or disclosure.
  • Controller: The legal entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.
  • Processor: A natural or legal person, public authority, agency, or other body which processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.

Article II: Categories of Personal Data Collected

In accordance with the principle of data minimization under Article 5(1)(c) of the GDPR, we only collect data that is strictly necessary for the execution of our contractual obligations and the delivery of event management services:

  • Identity & Account Credentials: Full legal name, unique username identifiers, encrypted authentication hashes, and verified email addresses.
  • Event Metadata & Content: Event titles, descriptions, venue location data, timestamp parameters, guest lists, and custom invitation responses (RSVPs).
  • Technical Telemetry & Log Data: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type and version, operating system architecture, referral URLs, and interaction timestamps captured via secure server logs.

Article III: Legal Basis and Lawful Purpose for Processing

We do not process personal data without an explicit legal justification. Under Article 6 of the GDPR, our primary legal bases include:

  • Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing required to instantiate user accounts, generate invitation links, and manage secure RSVPs.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Processing necessary for infrastructure security, fraud prevention, diagnostic log analytics, and platform optimization.
  • Explicit Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Voluntary opt-in permissions granted for non-essential cookies, specialized event marketing, or optional telemetry.

Article IV: Data Subject Rights and Enforcement

Subject to statutory exceptions, you hold comprehensive rights regarding your personal information under European and domestic privacy frameworks:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): The right to request certified copies of your personal data undergoing processing.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): The right to demand immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to Be Forgotten" (Article 17): The right to request complete deletion of your account records and associated metadata where retention is no longer legally mandated.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): The right to receive your structured profile data in a machine-readable, interoperable format (JSON/CSV).

Article V: Data Security Architecture and Breach Protocol

We deploy robust enterprise-grade security controls, including Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest within our relational database clusters, and strict role-based access control (RBAC). In the unlikely event of a security breach compromising high-risk personal data, we commit to notifying competent supervisory authorities within 72 hours in accordance with Article 33 of the GDPR.

Article VI: Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred internationally outside the European Economic Area (EEA), such transfers are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, ensuring equivalent legal protection standards.

Article VII: Amendments and Regulatory Updates

We reserve the unilateral right to amend this Privacy Policy to reflect evolving regulatory compliance requirements or structural platform upgrades. Material alterations will be formally communicated via prominent system notifications prior to taking effect.
